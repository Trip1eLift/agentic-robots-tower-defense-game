ARIA Unit Visual Identity

Each ARIA is 48x48 pixels. How should they read visually?

A

Armored Mech-Girls (Nier: Automata vibes)

Sleek feminine silhouettes with visible armor plating. Each class has distinct armor weight: Rex in heavy plate, Aurora in light recon gear, Lily in a flowing medic coat, Hana in an engineer jumpsuit with tool belt.

Color-coded by class: Red (Vanguard), Gold (Striker), Green (Medic), Blue (Architect)

Hair visible, 2-3 pixel eyes, small but expressive poses. Weapons held visibly.

B

Chibi Anime Robots

Big heads (1/3 of body), oversized eyes (3-4px), small bodies. Cute but combat-ready. Think Mega Man meets anime gacha art. Exaggerated proportions make each class instantly recognizable even at small size.

Rex: stocky wide stance, huge shield. Aurora: sleek with oversized rifle. Lily: gentle pose with glowing hands. Hana: goggles on head, wrench/hammer.

Very appealing, designed to make players attached to their units.

C

Hybrid: Proportional but Expressive

Balanced proportions (not chibi, not realistic). Heads are ~30% of body. Clear feminine form with class-specific gear. Each has a signature visual element: Rex's red scarf, Aurora's sniper scope visor, Lily's green cross emblem, Hana's blue hardhat.

Less cute than chibi, more grounded. Emphasis on silhouette readability -- you can tell the class from shape alone even without color.
